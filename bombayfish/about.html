College Field Project Overview

Title: “Fresh Fish Market: A Study of Offline Fish Selling and Development of an Online Fish Selling Website”

About Bombayfish: Bombayfish is a proposed online extension of traditional local fish markets. The platform is designed to make fresh fish availability, daily rate transparency, custom cleaning selections, and ordering convenient for urban consumers while empowering traditional dock vendors with structured digital order management.

Project Objectives

  • Study the traditional fresh fish selling process at local docks and retail markets.
  • Understand consumer purchasing behavior, quality assessment, and preference for cleaning/cutting.
  • Identify logistical, hygienic, and pricing challenges faced by traditional fish vendors.
  • Design and build a responsive e-commerce platform prototype (Bombayfish) tailored for daily seafood retail.
  • Evaluate how web technology bridges offline supply chains with online urban delivery demand.
